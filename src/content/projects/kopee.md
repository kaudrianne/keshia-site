## Why I'm building it

I use Beli to log restaurants, but I never log right after ordering. I log when I'm bored, and by then the coffee photo is lost somewhere in my camera roll. Talking to friends, I found everyone has their own workaround: one keeps a Notes list ranking cafes on a single drink, another doesn't keep a list at all and just posts to her Instagram story.
